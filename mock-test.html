2. Timing & Submission / समय और उत्तर जमा करना

  • The timer (top right) is server-controlled; remaining time appears top right.
  • The exam auto-submits when time ends—no manual submission required.

3. Language / भाषा

  • Questions are displayed in the language provided with the test. Use the language selector if available.
  • Language selection cannot change the question content if a translated version has not been provided.

4. Navigation / नेविगेशन (परीक्षा में चलना)

  • Use Previous or Save & Next to move between questions; use Mark for Review to flag questions you wish to revisit later.
  • After the last question, Save & Next moves to the next question or completes the test.

5. Answering / उत्तर देना

  • Each question has four options, out of which only one is correct. Select or change your answer at any time before saving.
  • Answers are saved only after clicking Save & Next.
  • To change a saved answer, revisit and update it, then save again.

6. Additional Notes / अतिरिक्त निर्देश

  • Maintain silence and do not communicate with other candidates.
  • The system saves responses for each question and auto-submits when time ends.
  • If you have any queries regarding exam content, seek assistance from the invigilator.
  • In case of a technical issue (system hang, network loss, or power failure), immediately inform the invigilator.
  • Bathroom breaks or leaving your seat are not allowed during the exam.
  • All exam materials provided must be returned before exiting the examination hall.
  • Do not attempt to capture screenshots or take photos of the exam screen.
  • Use only the designated computer and do not switch systems during the exam.
  • Any attempt to use unfair means or malpractice will result in disqualification.
  • Remain seated until the invigilator announces the end of the exam.
  • Do not discuss or share any questions or answers with other candidates.

Good Luck.
